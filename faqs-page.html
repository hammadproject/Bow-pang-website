FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS

How can I buy the comics?

Hit the shop here on bowbang.com for in-stock issues. Want new issues & wild exclusives? That's Kickstarter territory. Join the newsletter so you don't miss the chaos.

What is Bow Pang LLC?

Bow Pang LLC is an independent comic book publisher built on a simple idea: the world takes itself too seriously. Our story page.

Do you offer digital content?

We absolutely do. Digital issues live in the shop-easy to grab, easy to read, zero waiting.

How can I stay updated on new releases?

Start with the Bow Pang newsletter. After that, dive into our social hives of fun and infamy links waiting at the bottom of every page.

Who can I contact for collaboration or licensing?

For collaboration or licensing inquiries, please use the contact page on bowbang.com. Only submissions made through our designated forms will be reviewed; we do not accept or consider unsolicited ideas, scripts, or materials.
