Introduction

Bow Pang LLC is an independent comic book publisher built on a simple idea: the world takes itself too seriously.
‍

We create and publish fiction that leans into the weird, the playful, and the unexpected. Our stories are not here to teach lessons or follow rules. They are here to entertain, distract, and give people a break from everything else going on.
‍

Bow Pang LLC brings bold visuals and unapologetic energy into every page. This is a brand that values creativity over convention and fun over perfection.
‍

At its core, Bow Pang LLC exists to make people feel lighter, even if just for a moment.

Our Mission

At Bow Pang, we create comics and stories that spark imagination, celebrate creativity, and connect fans with worlds they love. Every page is crafted with passion, fun, and the unexpected.

Our Vision

Bow Pang aims to inspire imagination and creativity, building a universe where stories, characters, and worlds spark curiosity and wonder for fans everywhere.

We live and breathe comics, creating stories that inspire.

Crafting original characters and bold worlds that break boundaries.

Built for fans, by fans — we support and grow together.

Making a lasting impression on pop culture and beyond.

Bow Pang LLC – Bold. Weird. Fun. Original comics and stories that spark imagination, surprise readers, and celebrate the unexpected.
